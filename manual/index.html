NCC manual

NCC (Novation Circuit Companion) turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a two-part sound module for the Novation Circuit Tracks. The Circuit plays the notes and turns the knobs; the FM-1 makes the sounds and gives you the controls for playing live.

This manual follows NCC 0.4 (FM-1_704) and what is coming next. One difference to know about: in the next release you change pages with PLAY (tap for the next page, hold for the previous one); in 0.4 you turn SELECT instead. The longer, overlapping Drone transitions described below are coming next too. What changed when: CHANGELOG.md.

Hooking it up

MIDI. Send the Circuit Tracks' MIDI to the FM-1's TRS MIDI input, or use USB MIDI through a USB host adapter or a computer passing the MIDI on. NCC listens on:

Circuit Tracks trackMIDI channelFM-1 part
MIDI 13L (FM6 to start with)
MIDI 24R (JUNO to start with)

Use the Circuit's factory MIDI template on both MIDI tracks: its 8 macro knobs then send exactly the CCs NCC expects (1, 2, 5, 11, 71, 74, 12, 13). NCC follows the Circuit's clock and Start/Stop over USB (the default) or TRS; pick the source on GLO > SETUP, KNOB 4. With no clock coming in, the arp runs at the FM-1's own tempo.

Audio. Run a stereo-to-2×mono Y-cable from the FM-1's output into the Circuit's audio inputs 1 and 2. Part L comes out on the left only and part R on the right only, so each part arrives on its own Circuit input (see Split or stereo).

Then program notes on the Circuit's MIDI 1 and MIDI 2 tracks, press Play, and turn the Circuit's knobs.

The two parts

The FM-1 has two parts, L (blue) and R (pink). Each has its own engine, sound, arp, drone and sends.

  • OCT- puts the focus on L, OCT+ on R. The FM-1's 4 knobs and its buttons act on the part in focus; its keys play that part. This is the quick way to jump between your two sounds while jamming.
  • The Circuit always plays both parts, whichever has the focus.
  • The two parts share 8 voices.

Dual: play both sounds together

Dual lets one Circuit track play both parts at once: an arp on L and a pad on R from the same notes, for example.

  • Press SCL (the small button beside FX) to switch Dual on or off. The top bar shows D3 or D4 and the SCL light stays on.
  • D3 means the notes of MIDI channel 3 (the Circuit's MIDI 1) play both parts; D4 the notes of channel 4. The other channel's notes are ignored while Dual is on. The FM-1's keys play both parts too.
  • Choose CH3 or CH4 on GLO > SETUP 2, KNOB 1, or in the Manager. SCL remembers your choice.

Each part keeps its own sound, arp, HOLD and Drone, and OCT- / OCT+ still choose which part the panel edits. Dual shares notes only: the Circuit's knobs, pitch bend and sustain still go to their own part, so both sets of knobs keep working. Switching Dual on or off fades out what is playing; strike the keys again to carry on.

The screen

  • Top bar: the page (SOUND, ARP, an FX page ..), SPLIT or ST (stereo), D3 / D4 when Dual is on, and the tempo (amber when it follows the Circuit's clock).
  • The two part boxes: channel, engine and sound name of L and R, and two labels with the part's category and engine filter (PAD ALL). The part in focus is highlighted. A row of dots shows the steps of the bar.
  • KNOB 1–4: what the FM-1's own four knobs do on this page, with their values.
  • Bottom line: on the SOUND page, the focused part's category, engine and place in the list (TYPE PAD ENGINE ALL 3/10); on other pages a hint. When you turn a Circuit knob it shows the knob's label, what it does on that engine and the live value, for example R FILTER FREQ CUTOFF 2.1kHz.

Sounds and engines

NCC puts the sounds of all twelve engines in one library, sorted by what you use them for: BASS, PAD, LEAD, KEYS, PLUCK, BEAT. You choose a sound by its role; the engine follows. On the SOUND page (press HOME), three knobs, one job each:

ControlWhat it does
SELECT (knob)The category: ALL, BASS, PAD, LEAD, KEYS, PLUCK, BEAT (OTHER for names it can't place).
ALGORITHM (knob)The engine: ALL, FM6, JUNO, PHASE, TRIO, ACID, PHYS, GRAIN, VOICE, LOFI, WHEEL, NOISE, SAMPLE.
PRESETS (knob)The sound, among those of that category and engine. It loads once you stop turning, so you can scroll freely.
KNOB 1–4LEVEL, OCTAVE, VOICE (poly / mono / legato ..), GLIDE
  • The three knobs stop at the ends: turn back to go back. The bottom line says (FIRST) or (LAST).
  • While you turn, the screen shows in amber where you're going: the category or engine and how many sounds it has, or for PRESETS the name of the sound you'll land on.
  • When you change the category or engine, the part keeps its sound if it fits; otherwise the first sound that fits loads, so you hear the change right away. Combinations with no sounds are skipped (ACID has no pads).
  • Your own sounds join in: user presets and a DX7 bank in FM6 get their category from their name ("E.PIANO" is KEYS, "STRINGS" is PAD).
  • A newly chosen sound starts dry: its delay and reverb sends at zero, so it never arrives drenched in the effects of the sound before. Turn them up on FX > SENDS whenever you like.

There are 161 built-in sounds: 25 BASS, 40 PAD, 36 LEAD, 29 KEYS, 27 PLUCK and 4 BEAT.

PHYS, GRAIN and VOICE are heavier on the chip, so they play fewer notes at once (three or four per part).

More FM sounds: load a DX7 bank

FM6 plays real DX7 voices. Thousands of DX7 banks (.syx, 32 voices each) are around, for example the archive on Dave Benson's DX7 page. Drop one on the DX7 bank tab of the NCC Manager and send it to the FM-1: its 32 voices show up in the library under FM6, sorted by their names ("E.PIANO 1" is KEYS, "SYN-BASS 1" is BASS, "STRINGS 1" is PAD).

Coming soon: drum loops

A LOOP engine with lo-fi one-bar drum loops is in the works: always in time with the Circuit's bar, started by any note, chopped by the Circuit's steps, with shuffle, reverse, stutter and crush on the knobs. It is left out until it's done.

Some knobs work a little differently per model (LOFI's chips, PHYS's bodies): the popup always names what it does. Choose the model on EDIT. If you change a value on the FM-1 itself, your setting stays and the Circuit knob keeps working on top of it.

Arp and HOLD

  • ARP switches the arp on for the part in focus and opens the ARP page. Press ARP again on that page to switch it off.
  • KNOB 1–4: MODE (UP, DN, UPDN, RND, ORD, CHORD and more), RATE (1/4 to 1/32, triplets), OCTAVES, GATE. Page 2 (PLAY; in 0.4: SELECT): SWING, PROB, ORDER, DRONE (STACK / REPLACE).
  • The arp is always locked to the Circuit's clock, also when the tempo changes. A new chord starts on the next step, so it stays in time.
  • REC = HOLD: the arp keeps playing after you let go. A new chord replaces the held one; press REC again to release.
  • The Circuit's Stop stops the arp; its Start begins the pattern again from the top.

Drone

SEQ switches Drone on/off for the part in focus: notes keep sounding after you let go. It has two modes, chosen with KNOB 4 on ARP page 2, on GLO > SETUP 2, or in the Manager:

ModeWhat it doesBadge
STACKEach new note adds to the drone; play a note again to take it out.DR+
REPLACEPlay a chord and let go: the whole chord keeps droning. The next chord takes its place.DR1

In REPLACE you can add notes for as long as you keep at least one key down. Use VOICE = POLY (SOUND page, KNOB 3) to drone whole chords. With ARP on, Drone holds the arp's notes, so the arp keeps running over them.

Coming next: in POLY, with voices free, the old chord fades out while the new one comes in. Mono, unison, arp and full voice budgets use a slower fade out and in instead. The first chord starts straight away.

Press SEQ again to release the drone. The Circuit's Stop clears it too; Drone stays on for the next notes.

While FX is open, the white keys play performance effects instead of notes: beat repeats (1/8, 1/16, 1/32), reverse, filter sweeps, tape stop, freeze, octave shimmer, flanger, phaser and throws. Each lasts as long as you hold its key; the PERFORM page shows which key does what.

Throws, on every page: hold ENV for an echo throw, hold LFO for a reverb throw.

Split or stereo

  • SPLIT (the default): part L only on the left output, part R only on the right, so each part goes to its own Circuit input. The delay, reverb and chorus are shared by both parts, so their tails come out on both sides.
  • STEREO: both parts in stereo on both outputs, for headphones or a single input.
  • GLO + SEQ toggles between them; the top bar shows SPLIT or ST.

The FM-1 remembers your parts, sounds, effects and settings when you switch it off. A drone is not: it starts released.

NCC Manager (web page)

A web page to manage the FM-1 from your computer over USB, in Chrome or Edge: open the Manager and press Connect. The tabs:

  • Parts: what L and R play, their channel and filters.
  • Sounds: the whole library by category and engine, with a search box. L / R puts a sound on that part.
  • My sounds: the 32 user slots. Store what L or R plays now, load, rename, erase, and save them all to a file (or load such a file back).
  • DX7 bank: drop a 32-voice .syx, see its voices and their categories, and send it to FM6.
  • Backup: everything on the FM-1 in one file, and back.
  • Settings: MIDI channels, SPLIT or STEREO, the clock source, Dual and each part's Drone mode.

No FM-1 at hand? Add ?mock=1 to the Manager's address for a pretend one.

Installing

Installing firmware is at your own risk, but the way back is easy: the web installer's Return to official V15, or M-VAVE's own updater.

The easy way: open the web installer in Chrome or Edge, connect the FM-1 by USB (directly, with a data cable) and press Install.

The command line:

  1. Get the package: the newest ncc-X.Y.fwsc from the Releases page, or build it yourself (see BUILDING.md).
  2. Connect the FM-1 by USB and install it with the command-line installer (Python 3 with pip3 install mido python-rtmidi):
    python3 tools/fm1_install.py ncc-0.4.fwsc
  1. The FM-1 restarts into NCC. python3 tools/fm1_install.py --info shows what it runs (FM-1_704 for 0.4).

If an install is ever cut off, run it again, or hold OCT- while switching on to reach the updater.

Tips

  • Put a pad on L with Drone on, and an ACID line on R. Ride R's FILTER FREQ and RESONANCE on the Circuit while the pad rings.
  • Turn on Dual with an arp on L and a REPLACE drone on R: one Circuit track then plays a pattern over its own chords.
  • Tap FX once and you're on SENDS: a quick way to add delay to just one part.
  • Hold ENV on the last beat before a break for an echo throw.
  • Set SELECT to PAD and leave ALGORITHM on ALL: PRESETS then walks through every pad of every engine.
  • No FM-1 yet? Try NCC in your browser, even with your own Circuit plugged in.
