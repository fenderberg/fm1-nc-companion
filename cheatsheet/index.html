Cheat sheet

Every button on one page

Print it and put it next to your Circuit. One A4, landscape.

Download the PDF
NCC 0.4 Novation Circuit Companion for the M-VAVE FM-1 fenderberg.github.io/fm1-nc-companion

Hook it up

MIDI 1
channel 3: part L
MIDI 2
channel 4: part R
Out
Y-cable to the Circuit's audio in 1 (L) and 2 (R)
Template
the Circuit's factory MIDI template
Clock
over USB; TRS on GLO > SETUP, KNOB 4

Pick a sound

HOME
SOUND page
SELECT
category: BASS PAD LEAD KEYS PLUCK BEAT
ALGORITHM
engine
PRESETS
sound (loads when you stop)
KNOB 1–4
LEVEL, OCTAVE, VOICE, GLIDE
all three stop at the ends

Parts and pages

OCT-
focus on L
OCT+
focus on R
GLO
settings
EDIT
the sound's own parameters
PLAY
next page, hold: previous (0.4: turn SELECT)
GLO + SEQ
SPLIT ↔ STEREO

Play live

ARP
arp on, its page; again: off
REC
HOLD the arp
SEQ
Drone on / off
ARP p2 K4
Drone STACK or REPLACE
SCL
Dual: one track plays L and R
Stop
the Circuit's Stop clears arp and drone

Effects

FX
SENDS › DELAY › REVERB › CHORUS › INSERT › PERFORM › off
SENDS
DRIVE, CHORUS, DELAY, REVERB
Keys in FX
repeats, sweeps, tape stop, freeze
ENV hold
echo throw
LFO hold
reverb throw
New sound
starts dry

The Circuit's knobs

1 OSCILLATOR
the wave, the tone
2 OSC MOD
detune, feedback, movement
3 AMP ENV
attack + release
4 FILTER ENV
envelope to the brightness
5 FILTER FREQ
cutoff, brightness
6 RESONANCE
resonance, character
7 MODULATION
LFO, vibrato
8 FX
drive + send
middle = the sound as loaded
Lost? Hold OCT- while switching on for the update mode; the web installer can always go back to M-VAVE's firmware. The whole story: the manual.
