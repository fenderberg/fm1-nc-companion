NCCNovation Circuit Companion

Free firmware that turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a two-part synth for the Novation Circuit Tracks: every sound hands-on from the Circuit's eight knobs.

Install 0.4 Try it in your browserRead the manual Cheat sheet

WhyThe Circuit's spare pair

The Circuit Tracks has two synth tracks inside, plus two MIDI tracks and two audio inputs that often sit unused. That spare pair is an open invitation: plug in a second instrument and give each MIDI track its own sound.

The FM-1 is small, cheap and runs Felucca, a brilliant multi-engine firmware, but Felucca is a whole groovebox. NCC strips it down to one job: being the Circuit's companion. Less to learn, more to play.

What it doesFeatures

Two parts, two tracks

Part L listens to the Circuit's MIDI 1 track, part R to MIDI 2. The Circuit plays the notes and sends the clock; NCC has no sequencer of its own.

Split to its two inputs

L comes out on the left, R on the right: mix, mute and effect each part on the Circuit itself.

The Circuit's knobs just work

Its eight macros do what their labels say on every engine, smoothly, and the screen shows what each one does.

Sounds by role

SELECT picks the category (bass, pad, lead, keys, pluck), ALGORITHM the engine, PRESETS the sound. 161 sounds, and your own.

Twelve engines

DX7-style FM, a Juno, Casio CZ, a SID-ish trio, a 303, plucked and bowed strings, granular clouds, singing vowels, game-console chips, a tonewheel organ, noise and samples.

Dual: one track, two sounds

Tap SCL and one Circuit track plays both parts: an arp on one, a pad on the other, each with its own sound.

Arp and Drone

An arp locked to the Circuit's clock, with HOLD. Drone keeps notes ringing, or holds a whole chord until the next one crossfades in.

Effects one tap away

Sends, delay, reverb, chorus and an insert, performance effects on the keys, echo and reverb throws. New sounds start dry.

Dig deeper when you want

GLO holds the settings, EDIT the sound's own parameters, down to FM6's six operators. You never need them to play.

NCC Manager

A web page over USB: sounds on L and R, your own sounds, DX7 banks, backups and settings.

Coming soon: drum loops

One-bar loops that always fit the Circuit's bar, chopped by its steps, with shuffle, reverse, stutter and crush on the knobs.

QuestionsFAQ

What do I need?

An M-VAVE FM-1, a Novation Circuit Tracks, a MIDI connection to the FM-1's TRS input or USB (with a USB MIDI host adapter or computer), and a stereo-to-two-mono cable into the Circuit's audio inputs.

Can I try it before installing?

Yes, in your browser: the Try it page runs NCC itself. Plug your Circuit Tracks into the computer by USB and it plays the browser's FM-1, knobs, clock and all. How to hook it up.

Does it work without a Circuit Tracks?

Yes. The FM-1's keys play the parts, the arp runs on its own tempo, and any MIDI keyboard or sequencer on channels 3 and 4 works too.

How do I go back to the original firmware?

On the Install page, open “Return to official V15” and pick M-VAVE's FM-1.fwsc, or use M-VAVE's own updater. Your music is backed up first.

Which browser?

Chrome or Edge on a computer. The installer and the manager need Web MIDI with SysEx, which Safari and Firefox don't offer.

The arp runs out of time with the Circuit.

Press Stop and Start on the Circuit once: NCC counts its bar from the Circuit's Start, and follows its clock from there on.

Can I use my own sounds?

Yes: send DX7 banks to FM6 with the manager, and store your own sounds in its 32 user slots.

Is it safe?

It installs like M-VAVE's own updates, and the way back is always there. Still, installing firmware is at your own risk; if an install is cut off, run it again (hold OCT- while switching on for the update mode).

What is Felucca?

Felucca is Leo Kuroshita's open-source firmware for the FM-1. NCC is a fork of it, focused on being the Circuit Tracks' companion. Both are free software (GPL-3.0).

0.4

  • Twelve engines: PHYS, GRAIN, VOICE, LOFI, WHEEL, NOISE and SAMPLE join FM6, JUNO, PHASE, TRIO and ACID, all in the same sound picker and with the Circuit's eight knobs.
  • 161 sounds, 46 of them new.
  • Dual: SCL lets one Circuit track (channel 3 or 4) play both parts, each with its own sound, arp and Drone.
  • Drone REPLACE: hold a whole chord until the next one takes over. STACK works as before.
  • New sounds start dry: no leftover delay or reverb from the sound before.
  • GLO opens the settings, EDIT the sound's own parameters, down to FM6's operators.
  • The Manager has the Dual and Drone settings, and shows tempos above 127 BPM correctly.

0.3

  • Clearer sound picking: SELECT is the category, ALGORITHM the engine, PRESETS the sound, and all three stop at the ends. The part's box shows its category and engine.
  • Locked to the Circuit's bar: the arp can no longer drift off over time.
  • The LOOP engine is coming soon: it's left out while it gets finished.
  • A full 32-voice DX7 bank keeps its names after switching off.

0.1
